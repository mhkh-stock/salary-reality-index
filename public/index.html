These are NOT our community stats. They are published figures from other platforms, kept in a separate panel so they never inflate or mix with our own medians.

Why do the sources disagree?

Levels.fyi's median (about PKR 258,790/month for software engineers) sits far above PayScale (about PKR 80,000/month) and Indeed entry figures (about PKR 86,000/month) because Levels.fyi skews to top companies and counts stock + bonus, while the others skew broader and base-only. That is why every figure below shows its source, its sample size, and what it counts — so you can judge it yourself.

Honest limits: community medians come only from approved anonymous reports and are only as good as their sample size — small samples are labelled.
